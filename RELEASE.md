## v1.0.7 — 2026-11-22

- fix: **Upcoming no longer floods with "Event in …" rows on patch 12.1.** Each event now appears once, with its next start time.
- feat: the new 12.1 events **Curse Surge** (The Coiled Isle) and **Aqir Invasion** are shown with their proper names, under *Now* while running and under *Upcoming* before they start.
- fix: Upcoming countdowns now count down to when an event **starts** (they previously counted to its end), and events already running, such as Abundance, no longer appear in both *Now* and *Upcoming*.
